Behavioral Health Billing Challenges

Where Behavioral Health Practices Lose Revenue

The specialty-specific billing traps we fix every day for behavioral health practices.

Session Code Precision

90832 vs. 90834 vs. 90837 hinge on exact session minutes. We audit session lengths against billed codes so downcoding and takebacks stop.

Telehealth Modifier Maze

POS 02 vs. 10, modifier 95 vs. GT — telehealth rules still shift by payer. We keep payer-specific telehealth matrices current.

Medical Necessity Reviews

Payers probe treatment plans and session frequency. Our documentation checklists keep every chart defensible.

Authorization Limits

Session caps and re-auth requirements stall revenue. We track authorizations per patient and re-auth before the last covered visit.
