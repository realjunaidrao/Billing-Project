Expertise

Certified AAPC & AHIMA Coders, Not Data Entry Clerks.

The single largest cause of claim denials is incorrect coding. While many billing companies rely on untrained staff to simply re-type physician notes, Zxis Health employs strictly certified professionals.

Our team understands the nuances of complex specialties. We constantly monitor National Correct Coding Initiative (NCCI) edits, Local Coverage Determinations (LCDs), and evolving payer guidelines to ensure every modifier is placed correctly, preventing audits before they happen.

  • Strict adherence to ICD-10-CM guidelines
  • Accurate E&M leveling to prevent under-coding
  • Surgical and procedural CPT mastery
  • Continuous provider feedback loop
Technology

AI-Powered Scrubbing Meets Human Intuition.

Submitting a claim with an error guarantees a 30 to 45-day delay in your cash flow. We prevent this by passing 100% of your claims through our advanced rules engine before they ever hit the clearinghouse.

Our software catches demographic mismatches, missing authorizations, and incompatible code pairs instantly. But technology is only half the solution. When a complex denial occurs, our tenured A/R specialists take over, manually negotiating with payer representatives until the funds are released.

  • Pre-submission EDI 837 validation
  • Automated ERA (EDI 835) payment posting
  • Real-time eligibility API pings
  • 98.2% First Pass Resolution Rate (FPRR)
Security

Bank-Level Compliance & Data Security.

Your patients' Protected Health Information (PHI) is sacred. Zxis Health goes beyond standard HIPAA compliance to implement enterprise-grade cybersecurity protocols across our entire infrastructure.

From strictly role-based access controls to continuous internal audits, we ensure that your practice is insulated from data breaches and compliance penalties. We sign comprehensive Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) and maintain total transparency in our data handling.

  • End-to-End 256-bit AES Encryption
  • Strict Role-Based Access Controls (RBAC)
  • Routine 3rd-Party Vulnerability Scans
  • 100% HIPAA & HITECH Compliant
