Medical Billing for DME

DME billing means LCDs, capped rentals, modifiers for every rental month and prior auth on nearly everything. It’s the most rule-dense billing in healthcare — and we specialize in it.

DME Billing Challenges

Where DME Practices Lose Revenue

The specialty-specific billing traps we fix every day for dme practices.

LCD Compliance

Every DME item lives under a Local Coverage Determination. We check coverage criteria before delivery so claims don’t die on medical necessity.

Capped Rental Tracking

RR, KH, KI, KJ modifiers across 13 rental months — miss one and the claim denies. Our rental calendars track every month automatically.

Prior Authorization

Most DME needs auth before delivery. We run auth-first workflows so equipment ships only when coverage is confirmed.

PDAC & Coding Accuracy

Wrong HCPCS or missing PDAC verification means instant denial. We verify every code before the claim.
