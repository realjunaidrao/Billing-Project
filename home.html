Zaxis Pulse · AI Revenue Intelligence

The AI engine behind every clean claim

Zaxis Pulse is our proprietary AI platform — the same engine that powers our free revenue-leak review. It watches every claim the way a senior auditor would, flagging leaks before payers keep your money.

Pre-bill claim scrubbing

Every claim is checked against payer rules, NCCI edits and modifier logic before it goes out — denials stopped at the source.

Denial prediction

Pulse scores every claim for denial risk and tells your team exactly what to fix — before the payer says no.

AR prioritization

Worklists are ranked by collectible dollars and likelihood of payment — collectors always work the highest-value accounts first.

Underpayment detection

Paid claims are re-checked against contracted rates. When a payer short-pays, Pulse flags it for appeal automatically.

FAQ

Medical Billing Questions, Answered

Straight answers on pricing, coverage and how we work.

How much do your medical billing services cost?
Complete revenue cycle management is 5% of net collections — no setup fees, month-to-month after a 90-day ramp. A dedicated full-time billing specialist is a flat $1,500/month. Old AR recovery is 15% contingency: no recovery, no fee.
Do you work with new practices and startups?
Yes — startup billing setup is one of our specialties. We handle provider credentialing, payer enrollment, EHR setup and billing workflows from day one, so your first claims go out clean and revenue starts on schedule.
Which billing software and EHR systems do you support?
We are fluent in 89 billing and EHR systems including eClinicalWorks, AdvancedMD, athenahealth, NextGen, ModMed, Office Ally, DrChrono, Greenway, Tebra and CureMD. We work inside your existing system — no forced migration.
Which US states do you serve?
All 50 states. Our audited client portfolio spans 45 states with the deepest experience in California, Texas, Florida, New York and Arizona — and we take on new clients nationwide.
How does the free revenue-leak review work?
You send one billing export (aging report, denial report or claim dump). Our analysts map exactly where revenue is leaking — denials, underpayments, stuck AR — and return a findings summary. Free, no obligation.
How fast can you start working my accounts?
Most practices are live within 1–2 weeks of signing: system access, workflow mapping and a kickoff on your current AR. AR recovery projects can begin even faster.
