How the math works

Transparent Estimates, Real Benchmarks

No black box. Here's exactly what the calculator assumes:

Denial leakage

Denials above the 8% benchmark, of which ~65% are never reworked in a typical practice (widely cited industry figure). We model the winnable share conservatively.

A/R drag

Every day past 40 days in A/R locks a slice of annual collections in slow-moving receivables — cash you can't use and claims drifting toward timely-filing death.
