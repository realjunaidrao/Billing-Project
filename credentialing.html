Provider Credentialing & Enrollment

Avoid delayed payments and out-of-network denials. We handle the bureaucratic heavy lifting of provider enrollment so you can start billing faster.

The High Cost of Credentialing Delays

Provider credentialing is one of the most frustrating, time-consuming processes in healthcare management. A single error on a CAQH profile or a missed PECOS deadline can result in months of delayed payments, out-of-network penalties, and lost revenue.

Zxis Health assigns a dedicated credentialing specialist to your practice. We navigate the labyrinth of commercial payer applications, government enrollments, and facility privileging, ensuring your providers are fully enrolled and ready to bill on day one.

  • Accelerate payer contracting and enrollment timelines.
  • Eliminate application rejections due to missing data.
  • Prevent revenue loss from expired licenses or lapsed credentials.
  • Free up your internal staff from endless hold times with insurance reps.

What We Manage For You

  • Initial Credentialing: Getting new providers fully in-network.
  • Re-credentialing: Maintaining active status every 2-3 years.
  • CAQH ProView: Setup, data entry, and quarterly attestations.
  • Medicare (PECOS): Seamless 855I / 855R / 855B form management.
  • Medicaid Enrollment: State-specific portal management.
  • NPI & DEA Management: Registry setup and renewals.
  • Facility Privileging: Hospital staff application tracking.

Our Credentialing Workflow

A meticulous, highly organized process designed to get your providers enrolled as quickly as possible.

1

Document Gathering

We provide a secure portal to collect all necessary documents upfront: CVs, medical licenses, DEA certificates, malpractice insurance, and board certifications.

2

Application Prep & Submission

Our specialists accurately populate CAQH, PECOS, and specific commercial payer applications (BCBS, Aetna, Cigna, etc.), ensuring zero errors before submission.

3

Relentless Tracking

Submitting is only step one. We proactively follow up with payer credentialing committees every week to prevent your application from stalling on someone's desk.

4

Ongoing Maintenance

Once enrolled, we monitor your profiles. We handle quarterly CAQH attestations and set up automated alerts to renew expiring licenses before they affect your billing status.
