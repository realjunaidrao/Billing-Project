Physical Therapy Billing Challenges

Where Physical Therapy Practices Lose Revenue

The specialty-specific billing traps we fix every day for physical therapy practices.

The 8-Minute Rule

Timed-code units live or die on exact minute counts. Our charge workflows calculate units correctly so you never leave units — or compliance risk — on the table.

KX Modifier & Cap Management

Therapy cap exceptions need the KX modifier and medical-necessity support. We manage cap tracking per patient automatically.

Timed vs. Untimed Errors

Mixing timed and untimed codes in one visit is a classic denial source. Our edits catch it before the claim goes out.

Documentation-Driven Denials

Payers deny PT for thin daily notes. We give your therapists documentation checklists tied to what payers actually review.
