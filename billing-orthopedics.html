Orthopedics Billing Challenges

Where Orthopedics Practices Lose Revenue

The specialty-specific billing traps we fix every day for orthopedics practices.

90-Day Global Periods

Post-op visits bundled into the global package — unless modifiers 24, 25, 58, 78 or 79 apply. We catch every separately billable service.

Laterality Modifiers

LT, RT and bilateral billing errors are orthopedics’ most common denial. Our edits verify laterality on every claim.

Implant & DME Billing

Implants, braces and bone stimulators each bill differently. We separate surgical, implant and DME revenue streams cleanly.

Workers’ Comp & Personal Injury

Ortho carries heavy comp and PI volume with separate rules and fee schedules. We run dedicated workflows for each.

FAQ

Orthopedics Billing Questions

Straight answers for orthopedics practices.

Do you understand orthopedic global periods?
Completely — 10 and 90-day globals with correct use of modifiers 24, 25, 57, 58, 78 and 79.
Can you bill workers’ comp?
Yes, with dedicated comp workflows, separate fee schedules and authorization tracking.
Do you handle DME like braces?
Yes — orthotic and DME billing with LCD compliance, separated from surgical claims.
