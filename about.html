About Zaxis Health

Built by Someone Who’s Done the Work

Zaxis Health wasn’t started in a boardroom. It was built over a decade of chasing denials, working aged A/R, and learning exactly where the money hides.

The founder

Paul Wilson

Founder & Principal RCM Consultant

Everyone wants clean collections. I figured out how to make revenue leakage visible — before it’s written off.

After a decade inside the US revenue cycle — 264+ client folders audited, 45 states, 89 billing systems, 125 specialties — one pattern held: almost every practice is losing collectible revenue it can’t see in its own reports. That’s the problem Zaxis exists to solve.

Early on, I was the analyst doing revenue-cycle audits by hand — chasing denials, working aged A/R, learning where the money really hides. Then I ran RCM operations for 20+ clients with 6 team leads and 50+ staff, owning eligibility, coding, submission, posting and AR follow-up end to end.

Today I lead Zaxis Health, where I built an AI tool that turns a single billing export into a full revenue-cycle analysis — payer performance, denial patterns, AR aging by cause, and revenue leakage by CPT — in seconds.
