Why California Practices Switch to Zaxis

California practices deal with Medi-Cal managed care quirks, high claim volumes, and some of the toughest payer enrollment paperwork in the country. Here is how we fix the leaks that cost them most.

Multiple Medi-Cal managed care plans, each with its own authorization rules and billing quirks. We keep plan-specific workflows so claims go out right the first time.

California practices face aggressive downcoding and medical-necessity denials from large commercial payers. Our denial team appeals with payer-specific clinical language that wins.
