Primary Care Billing Challenges

Where Primary Care Practices Lose Revenue

The specialty-specific billing traps we fix every day for primary care practices.

Preventive vs. Problem-Oriented

One visit, two purposes — billed wrong, one side denies. We code the split correctly with modifier 25 where it’s earned.

Annual Wellness Visits

G0438/G0439 AWVs are pure found revenue most practices underuse. We build AWV programs that fill schedules and pay.

Chronic Care Management

99490 CCM pays monthly per qualifying patient — if time is tracked. We implement CCM time-tracking and billing.

E/M Downcoding

Payers shave E/M levels across high-volume primary care. We defend medical decision-making levels with documentation that holds.
